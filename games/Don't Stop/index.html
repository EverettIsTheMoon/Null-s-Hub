HOW TO PLAY

A/D or ←/→ steer   W/↑ gas   S/↓ brake
Space handbrake (hold to drift)   Esc pause
On a phone: touch the left/right side to steer and use the buttons.

Let off the gas and you slow down, and it catches up. Ramps make you fly. Crashes slow you down, and it is right behind you. Watch the mirror.
