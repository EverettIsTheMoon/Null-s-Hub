NIGHT DRIVE

Don't stop. Don't look back for long.
Steer: A/D or ←/→. Gas: W or ↑. Brake: S or ↓. Handbrake (hold to drift): Space. On a phone, touch the left/right side to steer and use the buttons. If you let off the gas, you slow down and it catches up. Ramps make you fly. Crashes slow you down, and it is right behind you.
